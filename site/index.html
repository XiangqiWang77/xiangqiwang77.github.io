How can a model learn from experience without losing the behaviors we trust?

My work connects continual learning, reinforcement learning, and adaptive inference. At Notre Dame, I’m advised by Prof. Xiangliang Zhang. I develop methods for efficient adaptation, reliable reasoning, and aligned decision-making.
